Dimension basis

Geometry traced from purchaser plan and foundation sheets, calibrated to the 31′ width and 40′ main depth. Room labels use the sell sheet’s nominal dimensions. The tape reads the scaled model. Ceiling heights, vault pitches, window heights, millwork, material appearance and mechanical placement are approximate. The two foyer risers are modeled at 7″ each, with a matching oak tread and painted risers. The kitchen tile backsplash is illustrative. The basement is unfinished with rough-in locations.
